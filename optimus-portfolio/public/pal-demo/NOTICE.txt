Portfolio adaptation (2026-10-10): one policy selected, portfolio navigation,
recorded evaluation tour, touch control additions, and static-only result storage.

standalone page preserves the original Policy Gym skin and shared layout.
The trained policy, scene, meshes, and saved evaluation are unchanged.
Featured policy: walk-flat-amp/k1_amp_legs_ref_turn. Trainer/exporter: Tund.
